ABOUT QUEVAI PROF

Meet the AI Professor built to teach visually

QuevAI Prof is an AI-powered educational tutor that combines artificial intelligence, an animated professor and an interactive whiteboard to make learning more visual and engaging

OUR IDEA

More than an AI chatbot

Traditional AI tools often give students paragraphs of text. QuevAI Prof takes a different approach.

When a student asks a question, the AI creates a structured teaching plan. Prof. Quev can then speak, think, point and interact with a whiteboard to explain the concept.

The goal is simple: make difficult concepts easier to see, understand and remember.

WHY QUEVAI PROF?
